Suffixes

A suffix follows a base. Derivational suffixes may change meaning or grammatical class; inflectional suffixes mark grammatical information without necessarily changing word class.

Suffix meanings vary by word. For instance, -er in teacher is different from -er in taller. The suffix -ly can also form adjectives, as in friendly. The element -logy is often described as a combining form.

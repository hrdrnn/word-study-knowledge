Prefixes

A prefix is a meaningful element placed before a base. Match the meaning in context; similar letters do not prove a morphological relationship.

Some Greek and Latin elements traditionally taught as prefixes are combining forms. For example, micro- and auto- carry lexical meanings. Label them accurately when needed.
